From street stall to platform

I come from a family of merchants and have built businesses in three areas: printing on clothing and promotional products; producing, distributing and selling food (cuernitos and tacos de canasta); and producing, distributing and selling fresh drinks, where I was a founding partner: I put in the capital, designed the operation from start to finish and then handed it over.

I have 20 years of experience in the public and private sectors improving operational processes through data analysis and process systematization. Alongside that, I spent 15 years teaching Programming, Data Analysis and Database Systems at IPN’s CIITEC, was a facilitator for SEP’s online high school program, and took part in launching Mexico’s New Mexican School (Nueva Escuela Mexicana) model.

I hold a degree in Industrial Management from IPN and a master’s in Learning Management in Virtual Environments from the University of Guadalajara. In 2006 I registered my first educational software, Guía Binaria, with INDAUTOR, Mexico’s copyright office: a program for teaching basic math.

Today I help small local businesses with sales strategies and digital tools, and I build EdTech training platforms that promote digital entrepreneurship and international mobility.

FAQ

What people ask before we start

What does “Partner with me” include?

I do not sell a website or a one-off automation. I design a complete strategy: I apply digital tools and prospecting methods to bring in customers and close sales. I focus on results, not proposals; so after assessing your business with no commitment, we agree in writing on how I charge: per lead or per closed sale.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Local businesses that already have a product or service that works and the capacity to serve more customers. I especially suggest service businesses, because they can grow and can also be taught: if you do nails or cut hair, why not teach it too? I work with only a few businesses at a time to give real follow-up.

How do we know which sales came from me?

Orders come in through the website, form or WhatsApp we set up, so every lead is recorded. That way we both see the same numbers and the agreement stays transparent.

Can I have my own learning platform too?

Of course! The idea is that you can pass on what you know how to do and earn from it. I help you turn your experience into a course or training platform, using teaching methods that work, and we can build a strategy to sell your course or training.

What is international mobility?

It is a strategy to grow academically, professionally or in your career in another country. The world needs what you know how to do, and you can benefit greatly from it. I share my own experience in the Work and Study program I am doing in Ireland. For paperwork and requirements, always check official sources.

Do I need to know about technology?

No. I handle the digital side and explain what you need to know in plain words. Teaching is part of my job.

How soon will I see results?

The first changes, like your Google Maps profile and WhatsApp, show within a few weeks. The full foundation is ready in one to three months, and from there we track your sales growth together.

Do you work remotely?

Yes, I work 100% online. Meetings are by video call and day-to-day contact is by WhatsApp, so it does not matter where your business is.

What is Wrealispec and when will it be ready?

It is a method to improve English with artificial intelligence and a teacher, designed for professionals and academics seeking international mobility. It is in development; if you are interested, leave your details in the form and I will let you know when it opens.

How can I help you?

Leave your details and I will get back to you within 24 hours to talk about your business or what you want to learn.
